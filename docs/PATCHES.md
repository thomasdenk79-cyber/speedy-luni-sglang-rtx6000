### QFN-0099 — [Feature] spec: Add a dynamic draft vocabulary ring

- Release: `rc2` · commit `50f3315bd21a` · 2026-11-17 · 4 files changed, 406 insertions(+), 3 deletions(-)
- Origin: OWN · GPU validation: pending · Upstream: deferred

A static --speculative-token-map misses topic tokens such as identifiers
that mostly appear in the prompt. SGLANG_SPEC_DYNAMIC_VOCAB_SLOTS=N appends
a FIFO ring of N non-hot tokens, taken from prompts and accepted outputs,
to the sliced draft head and scores them with the target LM head rows.

The EAGLE v2 worker stages prompt tokens at the prefill draft extend and
accepted tokens after verify, and commits them once per decode round
between the verify-side draft-probability scatter and the draft extend.

with, the draft model forward stays free of side effects, and the update
runs eagerly outside the CUDA graphs. The ring updates its buffers in place
and keeps target ids unique, so the captured draft head and hot_token_id
stay valid for rejection sampling.

The feature is off by default and needs a token map, a draft model that
uses the sliced head in place, an unquantized target head, and tp/pp/dp
size 1.

Adapted from the V3 dynamic draft vocabulary of
https://github.com/hyudryu/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP4-1xRTXPro6000 (MIT).

### QFN-0142 — hicache buffer: stream storage hits larger than the host arena

- Release: `rc2` · commit `4479f46fff2b` · 2026-11-18 · 7 files changed, 1320 insertions(+), 14 deletions(-)
- Origin: OWN · GPU validation: pending · Upstream: deferred
- Env flags: `SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM`, `SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_CHUNK_TOKENS`, `SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_DEVICE_HEADROOM`, `SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_H2D_MB_PER_ITER`, `SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_INFLIGHT`

Buffer-only HiCache staged a whole storage hit in one host bounce and
declined hits larger than the host pool (declined_host_oversize), so long
prefixes were recomputed after a flush. With SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM=1
such a hit streams instead:

- page-aligned chunks (SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_CHUNK_TOKENS) through a
  pinned ring of SGLANG_HICACHE_BUFFER_STREAM_INFLIGHT slots; the IO worker
  reads chunk k+1 (parallel file read workers) while chunk k is copied H2D
  on a dedicated lowest-priority stream; a slot is reused after its copy's
  event completed
- device rows are reserved per chunk by the scheduler, unpublished and owned
  by the plan, keeping DEVICE_HEADROOM free and evicting only unlocked nodes
- H2D is paced by a per-iteration quota (H2D_MB_PER_ITER), deposited by the
  scheduler tick right after a forward launch
- KV-derived sidecars ride the chunks; Mamba state is read after the last
  chunk and loaded at admission, when the rows are published into the tree
- all-or-nothing: a short read, deadline or abort frees the rows and the
  request recomputes; admission does not charge the resident rows again
- per-restore log line (tokens, chunks, SSD/H2D GB/s, overlap, step time vs
  idle baseline) and a time-to-publish line

Default off: no behavior change. Single-rank attention only.

### QFN-0166 — fix(mxfp8): harden the skinny GEMV gate, plan and scratch

- Release: `rc3` · commit `672fa14d50f7` · 2026-11-18 · 2 files changed, 985 insertions(+), 199 deletions(-)
- Origin: OWN · GPU validation: pending · Upstream: deferred
- Env flags: `SGLANG_ENABLE_QWEN_DEEPEP_SHARED_OVERLAP`, `SGLANG_MXFP8_SKINNY_GEMV_MAX_M`

- Files: `python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/mxfp8_skinny_gemv.py`

Round-1 findings on the MXFP8 skinny GEMV.

Gate and entry point share one validation of the operands, so they cannot drift
apart (B4). An empty batch and a degenerate N or K are refused instead of
reaching the kernel, where the broadcast path loaded row 0 of a zero-row
activation tensor unmasked and the planner divided by N or K; the kernel masks
that load and returns on M <= 0 too, so a direct caller cannot fault either.
Above MAX_M the rows are served by further chunked launches, so every row of
the output is written.

Split-K counters are reset on the stream for an eager launch (B3). A launch
aborted mid-kernel used to leave a counter non-zero, and the N block owning it
then never ran its fixup: the caller got a plausible partial sum with no NaN
and no error. Inside a capture the reset stays a no-op, which is what keeps the
kernel capture-and-replay safe; counters_are_zero() lets a test or a benchmark
check the invariant from the host.

The concurrency contract -- one in-flight split-K launch per (device, stream)
scratch, and a captured graph permanently owns its stream's scratch -- is
stated in the module docstring, and the registry is capped at _WS_LIMIT
instead of growing by 8 MiB per new stream (B5). A capture miss or a full
registry is logged once per (device, stream) rather than counted silently, so
a decode graph that stayed on the W8A8 GEMM is visible. prealloc() now also
covers the alt named stream the shared expert runs on and a caller's stream,
and the SM120 and SM-count caches are keyed per device (B2, B8).

MAX_TUNED_M clamps the gate to the widths the tables were measured at, so the
M = 17..32 rows no longer run M16 tiles with a 32-row masked BLOCK_M (B7), and
a pinned cfg that would not fit the scratch is demoted to SPLITS=1 instead of
writing past it. The tile tables name where they were measured
(kernels/microbench/w8a16v2/gemv_splitk_sweep.py) and that the entry, not the
comment, is authoritative (B6).
